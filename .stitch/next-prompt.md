A modern, premium luxury real estate Contact Page for Ankit Sarhadi and Kunal Sarhadi.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- **Theme**: Luxury, minimal, and premium.
- **Palette**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), Zinc-950 (`#09090b`), and Luxury Gold (`#D4AF37`) as the primary accent.
- **Typography**: `Outfit` for display headlines (clean, geometric) paired with `Geist` for body copy, avoiding any generic serifs.
- **Layout**: Grid-first, utilizing generous padding and spacing to convey elegance.
- **Anti-patterns**: No neon glows, no fake metrics, no 3-column equal grids, no overlapping elements.

**Page Structure:**
1. **Header:** Minimalist navigation (Home, Listings, Property Details, About Us, Testimonials, Contact) with a subtle Luxury Gold accent on the active link (Contact). Logo text "Sarhadi Real Estate".
2. **Hero Section:** A stark, elegant headline: "Private Inquiries" in Outfit font. Subtitle: "Connect with the architects of luxury real estate."
3. **Contact Layout:** A 2-column split.
   - Left side: "Direct Access". List the phone numbers, email (inquiries@sarhadirealestate.com), and physical address (e.g., 100 Wellington St W, Toronto) using sharp typography.
   - Right side: A minimalist, high-end booking/inquiry form. Use 1px Pure White bottom borders for inputs (Name, Email, Phone, Inquiry Details). Labels should be `label-caps` in Geist. Include a Luxury Gold "Submit Inquiry" button.
4. **Imagery:** A striking, full-bleed architectural image or a map view that matches the dark, desaturated aesthetic below or alongside the contact details.
5. **Footer:** Clean footer with navigation links, contact info, and copyright.
